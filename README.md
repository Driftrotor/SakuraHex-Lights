# SakuraHex Lights
Coz Lighting Doesnt Have To Be Boring!

<img width="8160" height="3768" alt="20261105_203849" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b5805cc-4f62-411f-a17e-70c25b819b8d" />

This Lighting is Designed for V2 350 Printers and i mocked this up as i wanted something a little more special that the usual lighing.
The Sakura Kumiko pattern adds a certain element to fun and elegance to the design and 
I love Kumiko Patternes!
I also wanted to keep this as cheap as possible, finding some 8mm wide cobb strips off Amozon for almost silly money.
Divided into 75mm long strips these can be stuck into the base parts and once soldered together to make the strings of your choosing the tops / diffusers snap over the top and succure the strips in place.
